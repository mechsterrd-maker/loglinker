Why a phone-based face attendance app beats a biometric machine

For years, tracking attendance meant buying a ₹15,000–₹40,000 fingerprint machine, paying for wiring and a yearly maintenance contract, and still fighting shared cards and broken scanners. A face attendance app removes all of that. Everything runs on a device you already own, and because it's your face — not a card or a finger on a shared pad — it's touch-free and almost impossible to fake.

  • Zero hardware cost — use any Android phone or tablet.
  • Set up in 5 minutes — no installation, no technician.
  • No buddy punching — recognition is tied to each person's face, with liveness checks.
  • Touch-free & hygienic — nobody shares a scanner.
  • Attendance becomes payroll automatically — no Excel at month-end.

How to download and set up the free face attendance app

The whole point is that anyone can do this — no IT skills required:

  1. Get the app. Open loglinkr.com/install on the Android phone or tablet you'll use at the gate, and install LogLinkr. Creating your account is free.
  2. Enrol your team. Take two or three quick photos of each employee's face. The app turns each into a secure faceprint linked to that person.
  3. Place the device at your entrance or reception.
  4. Start punching. Staff look at the camera for about a second and they're marked present — in and out.

From day one you have a clean, digital attendance record you can actually trust — and nothing to lose or argue over.

It's not just attendance — it does your payroll too

This is where a free attendance app saves you real hours. Because every punch is digital, LogLinkr counts present days, half-days, week-offs, holidays and overtime and works out each person's salary automatically — then sends every employee their payslip on WhatsApp. No printouts, no "how was my salary calculated?" arguments, no two lost days every month. Frequently asked questions

Is the face attendance app really free?

Yes — you get a full 45-day free trial with no credit card and no setup fee. Enrol your whole team, run attendance and payroll, and send payslips during the trial. After that, plans start at ₹99/month for up to 50 employees for the whole business (not per employee).

Do I need a biometric machine or any hardware?

No hardware at all. It runs on any ordinary Android phone or tablet. Mount a phone at your gate and staff punch in with their face — nothing to buy, wire, or maintain.

How long does it take to set up?

About five minutes: install the app, take two or three photos of each employee, place the device at the entrance, and start punching. No technician visit needed.

Is my employees' face data safe?

Yes. The app stores an encrypted faceprint (a set of numbers describing the face), not a gallery of photos, and uses it only to match attendance. You can delete an employee's data when they leave.
