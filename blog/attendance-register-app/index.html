Attendance Register App: Replace Your Paper Book (Free to Start)

Product 11 Aug 2026 · 9 min read · By the LogLinkr Team

The paper attendance register — the muster book, the khata, the ruled diary at the reception desk — has run Indian businesses for decades. It is cheap, familiar, and needs no electricity. It is also where money quietly leaks out: a late arrival ticked as on-time, a friend signing for an absent worker, a page torn out, and two hours of squinting at columns at month-end to turn it all into salary. An attendance register app keeps everything you like about the register — the simple in-and-out record — while quietly fixing everything that costs you.

This guide explains what a digital attendance register app actually does, how it compares to the paper book and to expensive biometric machines, and how you can move your whole team over in about five minutes using nothing more than a spare Android phone.

What is a digital attendance register app?

A digital attendance register app is simply the muster book moved onto a phone. Instead of a worker signing a ruled page — or an owner ticking names from memory — each person marks their arrival and departure on a device, and the app stores the entry with the exact time and, usually, the location. In a face-based app like LogLinkr, the worker just looks at the camera for a second and is marked present; nobody has to sign anything.

The important difference is not the screen — it is what the record becomes. A paper register is a dead record: you still have to read it, add it up, and copy the totals into a salary sheet by hand. A digital register is a live record: the moment someone punches in, the app already knows their present days, late marks, half-days and overtime, ready to become a payslip at month-end.

Why the paper register quietly costs you money

Most owners keep the paper book because "it works." It does — until you count what it costs. The three biggest leaks are:

  1. Proxy attendance (buddy punching): One worker signs for an absent friend. On paper it is almost impossible to prevent, and even a few faked days a month across a team adds up to real salary paid for no work.
  2. Errors and disputes: A late arrival rounded to "on time," an unreadable signature, a missed entry. At month-end these turn into arguments — "I was present that day" — that you cannot settle because the only evidence is a smudged page.
  3. The month-end maths: Turning a register into salary by hand — counting present days, deducting for absences, adding overtime — swallows a day or two every month and is exactly where costly mistakes creep in.

None of these are dramatic on any single day. That is precisely why they persist: each one is small enough to ignore and frequent enough to matter.

The whole appeal of an app-based register is that anyone can roll it out — no IT team, no technician visit. With LogLinkr the steps are:

  1. Install the app on any Android phone or tablet you already have.
  2. Enrol your team by taking two or three photos of each person's face — this is your new "register" of who works for you.
  3. Mount the device at the gate or reception where the old book used to sit.
  4. Start punching — employees look at the camera and are marked present, automatically and instantly.

What to look for in an attendance register app

Not every app is worth switching to. Before you move your team, check that it offers:

  • No hardware required — it should run on a normal Android phone, not force you to buy a device.
  • Proxy protection — face recognition with liveness checks, so a photo can't fool it, is far stronger than a shared PIN or card.
  • Built-in payroll — the register should become salary automatically, not just export a spreadsheet you still have to work on.
  • Multi-location support — if you run more than one shop, site or branch, you want every register in one dashboard.
  • A real free trial — enough time to test it properly with your whole team before you pay.
