The 7 things that separate a good attendance app from a bad one

Before comparing products, get your checklist right. When business owners regret their choice, it's almost always because they ignored one of these:

  1. Accuracy & anti-cheating. Can staff mark a friend present? Face recognition with liveness detection is the hardest to fake; shared PINs and cards are the easiest.
  2. Hardware cost. A biometric machine is ₹15,000–₹40,000 upfront per site, plus wiring and yearly maintenance. An app that uses a phone you already own costs nothing to start.
  3. Setup time. Some systems need a technician and days of configuration. A good app is live in about five minutes.
  4. Payroll, not just attendance. The real time-sink isn't marking present — it's turning attendance into salary. The best apps calculate pay automatically.
  5. Multiple locations. If you run more than one shop, site or branch, you want every location in one dashboard, not a separate register per place.
  6. Works for your staff type. Fixed-gate staff suit face punch-in; travelling field staff need GPS.
  7. Honest, all-in pricing. Watch for "free" plans that lock payroll or reports behind an upgrade, and always factor in 18% GST.

The main types of attendance app in India (2026)

Almost every option you'll find falls into one of four buckets. Knowing the bucket tells you more than any star rating:

  1. Face-recognition apps (no hardware). An Android phone or tablet at the gate identifies each worker by face. Best for fixed locations — factories, shops, clinics, offices — where you want strong anti-cheating and, ideally, built-in payroll. LogLinkr sits here.
  2. GPS / selfie apps. Staff mark attendance from their own phone with a location tag and a selfie. Best for field, sales and delivery teams who don't come to one gate.
  3. Biometric machines with an app. A traditional fingerprint or face device on the wall, paired with software. Accurate, but you carry the hardware cost, wiring and maintenance, and hygiene of a shared touch scanner.
  4. Full HRMS suites. Attendance is one module inside a large platform covering recruitment, leave, PF/ESI and more. Powerful for bigger companies, but often heavier and pricier than a small business needs.

How to choose in under five minutes

Run your business through these quick questions and the answer usually becomes obvious:

  1. Do your staff come to fixed locations? Yes → a face app. Mostly on the road → a GPS app.
  2. Do you want salary handled too? Yes → pick an app with built-in payroll, not attendance-only.
  3. Do you want to avoid buying hardware? Yes → a phone-based app, not a biometric machine.
  4. How many employees? Up to ~50 → an entry plan around ₹99/month; up to ~200 → a growth plan around ₹399/month.
